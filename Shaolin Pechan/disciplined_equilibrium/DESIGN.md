## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, disciplined, and refined aesthetic for an institution combining traditional martial heritage with clinical precision. Rejecting folklore pastiches and orientalist clichés, the visual direction articulates physical mastery, philosophical grounding, and anatomical exactitude through a contemporary, minimalist, architectural lens.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Disciplined Gravity:** Composed, authoritative, and deliberate, evoking both the silence of a courtyard and the rigorous focus of clinical practice.
- **Harmonized Vitality:** Traditional vermilion cinnabar—symbolizing sinew, vital energy, and institutional lineage—is balanced with warm golden ochre and mineral mustard accents, representing enlightenment, earth, and endurance.
- **Architectural Modernism:** Modernist proportions, immaculate whitespace, crisp structural framing, and calm stone-tinted surfaces create a space that feels quiet, elevated, and rigorously organized.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic aligns with contemporary architectural minimalism: clean bone-white and warm-stone surfaces structured by 1px hairline dividers, precise typography, understated depth, and intentional chromatic accents.

## Colors

The color palette establishes harmony between historical lineage and clinical clarity by counterpointing traditional vermilion with warm mineral ochre against a backdrop of warm stone neutrals.

### Strategic Color Roles
- **Vermilion Crimson (`#8F1D1D`):** The primary brand anchor. Used deliberately for primary call-to-actions, formal institutional seal points, critical certification marks, and high-priority states. Its deep tone prevents visual vibration while commanding respect.
- **Warm Ochre / Mustard (`#B87314` / `#C4820F`):** The secondary accent and distinction layer. Integrates across badges, level achievements, active navigational indicators, refined hairline accents, and curated tags. Ochre brings warmth, prestige, and organic grounding without competing with vermilion.
- **Deep Ochre (`#9E650C`):** Used primarily for accessible text on light ochre washes, high-contrast borders, and focused states to ensure AA/AAA compliance.
- **Amber Ochre Highlight (`#D49B28`):** Reserved for delicate highlights, hover enhancements on ochre elements, and micro-accents.
- **Structural Charcoal Slate (`#2B2D2F`):** Anchor for high-emphasis typography, dark borders, and structural framing.
- **Stone Surface Foundation:** `#FCF9F8` provides a warm, organic linen-paper backdrop, `#FFFFFF` serves as elevated card planes, and `#F4EFEB` / `#ECE6E0` define secondary containers and nested data grids.

## Typography

The typography couples architectural precision with clinical legibility.

- **Primary Editorial & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances geometric balance with human warmth. Its refined proportions give structure to curriculum syllabi, technical descriptions, and clinical analyses.
- **Technical Utility (Inter):** Deployed for dense information architecture, data readouts, metadata tags, and UI controls where neutral horizontal tracking ensures immediate parsing.
- **Archival Code Styling (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with a wide letter-spacing (`+0.08em`) to resemble catalog numbers, clinical records, or architectural markers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (8-column on tablet, 4-column on mobile) constrained to a maximum width of `1280px`.

- **Spacious Rhythm:** Vertical spacing is unhurried and confident. The rhythm alternates between tight groupings inside components (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) and broad expanses between major clinical modules or martial sections (`space-xl` to `space-xxl`).
- **Architectural Framing:** Columns and grid regions are separated by deliberate hairline margins, framing asymmetrical content balances (such as a 4-column master instructor profile alongside an 8-column historical curriculum timeline).
- **Responsive Handling:** Desktop margins of `3rem` taper down to `1.25rem` on mobile screens to preserve scan width for charts, anatomical maps, and data grids.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and synthetic glassmorphism, relying instead on tonal layering, crisp borders, and subtle ambient warmth.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FCF9F8` (Warm stone).
  - **Embedded Insets / Sections:** `#F4EFEB` or `#ECE6E0` with a 1px border.
  - **Raised Cards:** `#FFFFFF` bound by a 1px outline in `#E7DFD7`.
- **Atmospheric Lighting:** Shadows are soft, wide, and tinted with neutral umber rather than cold black.
  - **Resting Elevation (Level 0):** Pure boundary defined by `1px solid #E7DFD7`.
  - **Interactive Hover (Level 1):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 45, 47, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.04);` accompanied by an ochre-tinted border shift.
  - **Overlays / Drawers (Level 2):** `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(28, 27, 27, 0.12);` paired with a translucent `#1C1B1B` backdrop at 45% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`4px` base border radius).

- **Architectural Edges:** A disciplined 4px radius (`rounded-sm` / default) grounds primary cards, input fields, and standard buttons, evoking carved timber and clean-cut masonry.
- **Pill Exception for Tags & Chips:** Full circular radiuses (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for status badges, syllabus tags, and active ochre filters, instantly distinguishing metadata and taxonomy chips from structural containers and interactive buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Vermilion Action):** Background `#8F1D1D`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 4px. On hover, deepens to `#6E1313` with subtle transition. Active state introduces a warm ochre outline ring (`2px solid rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Ochre Distinctive):** Background `rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #B87314`, text `#9E650C`, radius 4px. Hover transitions to `#B87314` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for secondary certifications, syllabus downloads, and tier reviews.
- **Outlined Neutral:** Transparent surface, `1px solid #2B2D2F`, text `#2B2D2F`. Hover fills with `#2B2D2F` and white text.
- **Ghost Button:** Text `#8F1D1D` or `#9E650C`, transparent background, underlined only on hover.

### Badges, Tags & Active Indicators
- **Ochre Discipline Badges:** Background `rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.35)`, text `#9E650C`. Rendered as full pills (`rounded-full`) with `label-caps`.
- **Lineage / Seal Badges:** Background `rgba(143, 29, 29, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(143, 29, 29, 0.25)`, text `#8F1D1D`.
- **Status Chips & Active Tabs:** Inactive states feature `#F4EFEB` background with `#58524D` text. Active state adopts a crisp ochre indicator: `#B87314` bottom border (2px) or a soft `#B87314` wash with deep ochre text.

### Cards (Course, Treatment & Syllabus)
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by `1px solid #E7DFD7`.
- **Header Accents:** Cards feature an optional hairline top border in ochre gold (`#B87314`) to denote advanced master-level modules or specialty medical clinics.
- **Hover Behavior:** Border transitions from `#E7DFD7` to `rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.6)`, accompanied by a subtle Level 1 ambient warmth shadow.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height: 44px, radius 4px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7DFD7`, text `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#8F1D1D` with a warm glow ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 130, 15, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp 16px geometric elements with a 2px radius (checkbox) or circular boundary (radio).
- Default: Border `1.5px solid #8A8179`.
- Checked: Vermilion `#8F1D1D` fill with white checkmark, bordered with a subtle `#B87314` focus ring when navigated via keyboard.

### Lists & Data Matrices
- Clinical logs, technique syllabi, and student ranks use horizontal dividers (`1px solid #E7DFD7`).
- Hover shifts the row background to `rgba(184, 115, 20, 0.04)`.
- Left-edge active indicator: A vertical 3px ochre bar (`#B87314`) marks the selected or currently viewed entry.
