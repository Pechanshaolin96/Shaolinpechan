## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, disciplined, and refined aesthetic for an academy uniting traditional Chinese martial arts and traditional Chinese medicine (TCM). The visual direction rejects orientalist pastiche, faux-calligraphic fonts, and faux-parchment textures. Instead, it translates centuries of clinical rigor and physical mastery into a contemporary, minimalist, architectural medium.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Disciplined Precision:** Clarity, focus, and deliberate placement reflecting internal conditioning and anatomical exactitude.
- **Clinical Calm & Vitality:** Grounded, unhurried space paired with an intense, disciplined vermilion cinnabar accent representing life force (Qi) and institutional lineage, complemented by a warm amber-ochre tertiary tone.
- **Architectural Poise:** Balanced grids, structured asymmetry, and generous white space reminiscent of temple courtyards and modern medical pavilions.

### Visual Language
The interface combines Modernist Swiss structural discipline with warm stone minimalism. Contrast is kept high for legibility, with razor-thin structural dividers (1px) framing content like tatami mats or courtyard pillars. Surfaces feel tactile yet digital, using immaculate neutrals, bone-white cards, and singular points of deep cinnabar focus.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical cleanliness with the gravitas of historical lineage. 

- **Primary (`#8B181A` — Cinnabar / Vermilion):** Reserved strictly for focal actions, key statuses, marks of certification, and dynamic callouts. It evokes cinnabar seals, sinew vitality, and traditional acupuncture locus points without shouting.
- **Secondary (`#2B2D2F` — Deep Slate/Soot):** Used for structural chrome, high-emphasis text, technical counters, and secondary interactive actions.
- **Tertiary (`#D99236` — Warm Amber / Herb Ochre):** Used for botanical/herbal indicators, subtle category tagging, metadata, and warm accent highlights.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Canvas Background: `#F8F9FA` (Soft cool-stone white)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - Subdued Surface: `#F1F3F5` (Subtle muted container fill)
  - Border System: `#E5E7EB` (Crisp 1px line separator)
  - Text Dominance: Primary `#1A1A1A`, Muted Secondary `#5E646A`, Subdued `#8C939A`.

## Typography

The typography leverages modern sans-serif fonts to project contemporary discipline, medical clarity, and athletic rigor.

- **Primary Voice (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric balance and disciplined terminal cuts. It brings approachable authority to titles and clinical legibility to long-form anatomy, diagnosis, and martial syllabus texts.
- **Operational Utility (Inter):** Applied across micro-copy, data matrices, labels, medical schedules, and clinical badges where structural neutrality is paramount.
- **Rhythm & Uppercase Accents:** `label-caps` enforces high tracking (`+0.08em`) in full uppercase, functioning like an architectural stamp or cataloging code (e.g., `ACUPUNCTURE MERIDIAN L-4`, `WUSHU KUEN`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are rooted in an intentional 12-column grid system on desktop (4-column on mobile, 8-column on tablet) centered within a maximum container width of `1280px`. 

- **Breathing Space & Stance:** Spacing derives from martial arts stances (Ma Bu)—wide, grounded, and deliberate. Ample vertical rhythm (`space-xxl` between major thematic domains) ensures clinical and educational content does not feel dense or overwhelming.
- **Grid Discipline:** Elements strictly snap to grid columns. Asymmetrical balances—such as an 8-column clinical syllabus paired with a 4-column master instructor profile—must align with 1px border dividers spanning full column heights.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Desktop margins of `3rem` contract to `1.25rem` on mobile, while gutter spacing shifts from `1.5rem` to `1rem` to conserve horizontal screen real estate for diagrams and medical case notes.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, blur rings, and artificial 3D skewing, adhering instead to flat architectural layering and low-contrast outlines.

- **Crisp Structural Outlines:** Hierarchy is primarily defined by `1px solid #E5E7EB` dividers, evoking modern shoji screens and sterile clinical precision.
- **Ambient Elevation (Hover & Focus Only):**
  - **Surface Default (Level 0):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8F9FA` stone canvas, bounded by a 1px border.
  - **Elevated Card (Level 1):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03);`
  - **Interactive Modal / Popover (Level 2):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08);` with a subtle `#1A1A1A` backdrop overlay at 40% opacity.
- **Surface Layering:** Embedded sections (e.g., student rosters, herbal formula details) employ `#F8F9FA` or `#F1F3F5` tonal fills rather than higher elevation shadows to maintain an understated, clinical profile.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`4px` base border radius).

- **Precision Corners:** A subtle `4px` radius softens crisp rectangular forms while preserving the authoritative, architectural feel of stone plinths and clinical logs.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Rounded pill profiles are exclusively reserved for category markers, medical status tags, and inline badges (`rounded-full`) to immediately demarcate functional meta-labels from structural cards and buttons.
- **Card Containers:** Bound by `rounded-md` (`6px` to `8px`) with an invariant `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#8B181A` background, pure white text, 4px border radius. Hover introduces deep crimson saturation (`#731315`), accompanied by a 150ms ease transition. Padding: `10px 20px` (`label-md`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with `1px solid #1A1A1A` border and `#1A1A1A` text. Hover transitions to `#1A1A1A` fill with white text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text-only with `#1A1A1A` or `#8B181A`, underlined only on hover.

### Badges & Meta Tags
- **Standard Tag:** Height 24px, uppercase `label-caps`, padding `2px 8px`, border radius `9999px` (pill). Neutral background `#F1F3F5` with `#2B2D2F` text.
- **Academy/Lineage Badge:** Muted Cinnabar tint background (`rgba(139, 24, 26, 0.08)`), text `#8B181A`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(139, 24, 26, 0.2)`.

### Cards (Course, Treatment, & Syllabus)
- Flat pure `#FFFFFF` background encased in `1px solid #E5E7EB` border.
- Header sections feature a crisp bottom border dividing title meta from curriculum body.
- On hover: Border color transitions from `#E5E7EB` to `#8B181A`, coupled with subtle Level 1 ambient elevation.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height: 44px with a 4px corner radius.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, typography `body-md`.
- Active / Focus: Border changes to `#8B181A` with a 2px soft outer focus ring (`rgba(139, 24, 26, 0.15)`).

### Lists & Data Grids
- Clinical formulation sheets and syllabus lists feature border-bottom row dividers (`1px solid #E5E7EB`).
- Row hover reveals `#F8F9FA` background with smooth transition.
- Numerical index or sequence tags displayed using uppercase `Inter label-caps`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Meridian / Anatomy Blueprint Viewers:** Clean white wireframe panels on subtle stone backgrounds featuring cinnabar pin-indicators.
- **Curriculum Stage Indicator:** A horizontal sequence of low-profile segments connected by hairline borders, highlighting current martial/clinical rank with a solid vermilion indicator.
